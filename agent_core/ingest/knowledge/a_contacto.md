## Contact details

RG Wallcovering can be reached by email at **info@rgwallcovering.com** and by
phone at **+1 (401) 722-9255**. The business is based in **Providence, Rhode
Island 02940**.

The website carries the line "Turning your walls into works of art".

## What the website offers as ways to get in touch

The site lists an email address and a phone number. There is no online
booking, and no quote calculator — consistent with work being priced per
project after the space is understood.

## Sections of the website

Home, About Us, Services (Commercial and Residential), Interior Design,
Portfolio, Blog and Contact. The Portfolio section shows completed residential
and commercial projects as a photo gallery.
