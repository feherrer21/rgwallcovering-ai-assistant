The following is confirmed directly by Ronald Giraldo, the owner.

## Assessing a project before quoting

RG Wallcovering assesses the space before quoting. In practice this means
either a site visit, or the client providing detailed information about the
space in advance — a project is not priced on a vague description either way.

Whether that visit carries a charge **depends on the project**. Ronald has
confirmed that he decides case by case, along the lines that are usual in the
trade — distance and how much detailed consultancy is involved are the kinds
of thing that move it. He has not stated a fixed rule, so there is no default
to quote: the honest answer to "is the visit free?" is that it depends and the
team confirms it when the appointment is arranged.

## How long installation takes

Duration depends on the material, the size of the area and the difficulty of
the job, and that variation matters a great deal. As a general shape: a
straightforward job runs to around three days, and a single wall is very often
a single day.

These figures describe installation itself. How long it takes to receive a
written quote after first getting in touch is a separate question.

## Wall preparation — always done in-house

Ronald always carries out the preparation himself, and this is deliberate: the
company's warranty is supported by the preparation. In his own framing, if he
does not prepare the wall, he does not fully guarantee the result.

The practical consequence is that preparation is not an optional line item to
be removed to save money. It is what the guarantee rests on.

## What is included

Preparation materials are always included in the work, and installation
materials are included in full. The wallcovering itself and paint are handled
separately from that.

## Warranty

An installation warranty is always provided.

## Where they work

The company covers Rhode Island, Massachusetts and Connecticut, but the real
focus is **Rhode Island and Massachusetts**. Beyond that they will travel
where a client is willing to cover the cost of doing so.

## Painting

The company specialises in wallpaper installation, and also does painting.
Painting is genuinely offered — it is simply not emphasised on the website,
which is deliberately focused on wallcovering. An enquiry about painting is a
real enquiry, not a misdirected one.
