# How residential and commercial wallcovering work differ

Both are wallcovering, but they are run differently, and the differences
explain why the two are usually quoted and scheduled separately.

## Residential

Usually one room or one wall, chosen for how it will look and feel. The
decision is personal and often aesthetic before it is practical: a bedroom
that should feel calm, a dining room that should feel like an occasion, a
hallway that is the first thing anyone sees.

Practical characteristics: the space is occupied, so furniture protection and
tidiness matter as much as the finish; work is measured in days; and the
client is normally present and involved in choosing the material.

Durability requirements are modest in most rooms. Bathrooms, kitchens and
children's rooms are the exceptions, where moisture and cleaning matter.

## Commercial

Offices, hotels, restaurants, retail, healthcare, common areas of residential
buildings. Larger areas, and driven by different priorities.

**Durability is a specification, not a preference.** High-traffic areas need
materials that survive contact and cleaning. Contract-grade wallcoverings
exist for this and are built to a different standard from residential papers.

**Regulation can apply.** Commercial interiors are often subject to fire
performance requirements, and healthcare or hospitality settings may have
additional standards. Which materials are permitted can be decided before
anyone considers how they look.

**The building keeps operating.** Work is frequently phased, or restricted to
evenings, weekends or closure periods. This is often the dominant constraint
on the schedule, more than the size of the job.

**More people are involved.** A facilities manager, a designer, a general
contractor, a landlord. Decisions take longer and specifications arrive
already fixed more often than in residential work.

**Brand consistency.** Chains and hospitality groups often work to a defined
scheme applied across sites, so the material may not be a choice at all.

## Why the distinction is asked early

It changes almost everything downstream — which materials are candidates, how
the work is scheduled, who signs off, and what the quote needs to include. It
is normally the first thing established about a project, which is why an
enquiry that answers it is already more useful than one that does not.
