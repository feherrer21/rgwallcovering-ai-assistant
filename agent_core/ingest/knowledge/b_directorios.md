The following details come from third-party business directories. They are
attributable but have not been confirmed by the business, and directory
listings can be out of date.

## Legal identity

The full registered name is **RG Wallcovering & Painting, Inc.** The company's
principal is **Ronald Giraldo**. The mailing address on record is PO Box
41447, Providence, RI 02940. A second phone number, (401) 359-7398, appears
alongside the main line of (401) 722-9255.

## Trading history and size

The business started on **22 October 2006** and was incorporated in 2017,
giving it roughly nineteen years of trading history. It has **five
employees**. It holds Rhode Island contractor licence number 35269. It is
rated **A+ by the Better Business Bureau**, though it is *not* BBB accredited
— the company has not applied for accreditation, which is a different thing
from failing to obtain it.

## Service area

Directory listings show work across Rhode Island — Providence, Cranston, East
Providence, Cumberland, Cumberland Hill, Coventry, North Providence, Johnston,
Lincoln, Greenville and Foster — and also in **Massachusetts and
Connecticut**. The company describes itself as serving New England.

## Services listed

Directories list a wider range of services than the website presents:

- Wallcovering and wallpaper installation
- **Wallpaper removal**
- Wall murals
- **Interior and exterior painting**
- Wallcovering sales

The painting side of the business is substantial enough to appear in the
company's registered name, but rgwallcovering.com presents almost exclusively
as a wallcovering brand.

## Reviews

is too small a sample to describe as a rating and should not be presented as
